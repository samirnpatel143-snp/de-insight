About DE Insight

Engineering knowledge, explained practically.

DE Insight is a learning platform and YouTube channel focused on making data engineering concepts easier to understand and apply.

The mission

Learn. Build. Engineer.

DE Insight focuses on practical learning: not only what a technology is, but how it fits into a real data platform.


The goal is to connect fundamentals such as SQL and Python with production concepts such as ETL/ELT, orchestration, cloud platforms, observability and data architecture.

What DE Insight covers

  • Data engineering fundamentals
  • Practical projects and pipelines
  • Cloud and modern data platforms
  • Informatica IDMC / IICS
  • Architecture and interview preparation
  • Technical tutorials and resources
